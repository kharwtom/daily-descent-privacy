Summary

Daily Descent is a single-player game for Android. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data about you.

What the app stores on your device

The game keeps the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your saved run and your daily results (run number, win or loss, floor reached, kills, HP left, turns and the path you took). Your streak is counted from these results;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, language, whether you finished the tutorial and whether you have seen the intro;
  • a time anchor: a timestamp from the network, together with the device's uptime clock and its restart counter. The game uses it to know which UTC day it is without trusting the device clock.

None of this is sent to the developer. It stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Network use

The game makes one kind of network request: it asks time servers for the current time. It does this on the first launch, when you open or return to the app, and again after your device restarts. The servers it may contact are time.google.com, pool.ntp.org and time.cloudflare.com, using the standard NTP protocol.

Like any internet request, this one shows your device's IP address to the server that answers it. The operators of those servers (Google, the volunteers who run the NTP Pool, and Cloudflare) handle that under their own terms. The game adds no identifier of its own and sends none of your game data. The developer does not run these servers and does not see their logs.

If the time check fails, the game keeps using the last time it knows while that time is still valid. Otherwise it asks you to try again.

Backup

The app allows Android's own backup. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may copy the saved game (not the time anchor) to your Google account, and may move it to a new device when you set one up. Android and Google do this under your system settings, not the developer, who cannot read these copies. You can turn backup off in your device's system settings. To remove an existing backup, use your Google account or your device's backup settings.

Sharing a result

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share on the result screen. The game then builds a short text (run number, the path and floor you reached, kills, HP left, whether it was your first try, and your streak) and hands it to Android's share sheet. You choose the app and the recipient. The developer never sees the text or where it goes. The text contains no name, account or device identifier.

Permissions and third parties

The app asks for the internet permission, which the time check needs. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The game contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting library, and no library that sends data about you to the developer or to a third party for its own use. The libraries it uses draw the game, play sound, store progress on the device, read the time and open the share sheet. The only network traffic is the time check described above.

Google Play and Android can give the developer anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from devices whose owners chose to share diagnostic data with Google. That is a Google service outside the app, covered by Google's own privacy policy, as is installing the app from Google Play.

Children

Daily Descent is not directed at children under 13, and the developer collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your choices

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing on a server to request, correct or delete. To delete the data on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Turning off your network connection stops the time requests. The app then keeps using its last valid time check, and only the first launch, or the first launch after a device restart, waits for a time check to succeed.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page is updated and the effective date at the top changes.
